# Finance Reference

Practical reference material for understanding personal-finance concepts, products, risks, and
decision checks. These guides are explanatory resources rather than interview Q&A.

[Back to career resources](../README.md)

> This material is educational, not individualized financial, tax, insurance, or legal advice.
> Product terms, tax rules, and regulation change. Check the applicable financial year, assessment
> year, tax regime, current product document, and regulator before acting.
