## Cash Reserve And Deposit Products

An emergency fund is liquid money for income interruption and unexpected essential costs. Three or
six months of expenses can be a starting scenario, not a universal answer. Base the amount on
dependants, employment stability, deductibles, debt obligations, access to credit, and the likely
time needed to replace income.

A **fixed deposit (FD)** places money for a defined tenure at stated terms. A **recurring deposit
(RD)** accepts periodic deposits over a defined tenure. Check premature-withdrawal rules, penalties,
renewal defaults, tax, whether the interest rate is fixed or variable, and deposit-insurance coverage.

A **flexi deposit** or **auto-sweep** links a transaction account to term deposits and moves balances
above or below configured thresholds. It can improve yield on idle cash, but the source document's
claim of a guaranteed 5-7% is unsafe. Rates, sweep lot sizes, tenure, breakage method, tax, and the
order in which deposits are liquidated vary by bank.

## Interest Rates And RBI Tools

The **policy repo rate** is the rate used in RBI liquidity operations against eligible collateral.
Changes can influence market and lending rates, but pass-through is neither immediate nor identical
for every loan or deposit.

A **fixed interest rate** remains fixed for the contractually specified period. A **floating rate**
changes according to its benchmark and reset terms. For a loan, compare benchmark, spread, reset
frequency, conversion fees, prepayment rules, and whether a rate change alters the EMI, tenure, or
both.

The **Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR)** is the prescribed share of a bank's net demand and time liabilities
maintained as cash balance with RBI. The **Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)** is the prescribed share
maintained in specified liquid assets by the bank. Both are regulatory/liquidity concepts, not rates
paid to a retail depositor.

## Banks And NBFCs

A non-banking financial company performs specified financial activities without being a bank. The
source claim that NBFCs are “not regulated” was wrong: RBI registers, regulates, and supervises
applicable NBFC categories under its statutory framework. Regulation does not make every NBFC
product equivalent to a bank deposit, and only eligible entities may accept public deposits.

Before placing money with or borrowing from an NBFC, verify the legal entity and RBI registration,
whether the proposed activity is permitted, grievance route, charges, security, credit risk, and
whether any deposit insurance applies. Do not rely on an app name alone.

## Credit Cards And E-Mandates

A credit card is revolving credit with a billing cycle, due date, minimum amount due, interest,
fees, and a credit limit. Paying only the minimum can leave most of the balance accruing expensive
interest. Compare total cost and terms before optimizing rewards such as travel, movies, or golf;
reward programs and insurance benefits change frequently.

An **e-mandate** authorizes recurring electronic debits within the mandate's terms. Review merchant,
amount or cap, frequency, end date, pre-debit notice, cancellation method, and failed-payment rules.
Periodically inspect active mandates instead of assuming that uninstalling an app cancels them.

## Health Insurance Checklist

Health insurance transfers specified medical-cost risks subject to the policy wording. Instead of a
static insurer list, compare the current policy and insurer on these dimensions:

- sum insured, family structure, restoration and cumulative-bonus rules;
- co-payment, deductible, room-rent or category limits, zone restrictions, and disease sub-limits;
- exclusions and waiting periods, including pre-existing disease and specified procedures;
- inpatient, pre/post-hospitalization, daycare, domiciliary, ambulance, and outpatient terms;
- cashless network and the process for reimbursement, preauthorization, complaints, and portability;
- renewal, migration, premium revision, age bands, and continuity when leaving an employer plan; and
- accurate disclosure of health history, because non-disclosure can affect a claim.

“No co-pay” or “no room-rent cap” can be desirable, but higher premium and other restrictions still
matter. Read the customer information sheet and full policy wording.

## Term Insurance And ULIPs

**Term life insurance** pays the stated benefit when a covered death occurs during the policy term,
subject to the contract. Cover needs should be estimated from dependants' future expenses, debts,
existing assets, education or care obligations, inflation, and the support period. “Twenty times
income” is only a screening rule.

A **ULIP** combines life cover with market-linked investment funds. It is not automatically a bad
or good product. Compare mortality and other charges, lock-in, surrender rules, fund options,
switches, insurance amount, tax treatment, and a transparent alternative of separate term cover and
investments. Do not treat an illustration as a guaranteed return unless the contract explicitly
provides a guarantee.
