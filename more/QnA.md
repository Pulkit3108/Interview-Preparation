How do you make estimations for daily tasks

When estimating daily tasks, I follow a structured approach to minimize underestimation:

Break Down Tasks: Divide the work into smaller, manageable pieces and estimate time for each.
Add Buffers: Multiply the total estimate by 1.5 to account for testing, reviews, and minor delays.
Double It: Double the result to prepare for larger, unforeseen challenges.

Why? Because estimates often miss hidden factors: time to build, test, and deploy; code reviews; changes in requirements; delays from dependent teams; compliance checks; holidays; or even team members falling sick. For instance, I once estimated a task to take a week, but with unexpected integrations and revisions, it stretched to four weeks. Experiences like these help me factor in real-world complexities for more accurate estimations.
